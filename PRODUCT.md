# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Customers shopping for traditional jewellery, bridal sets and gifts within India, on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

A complete custom Saalankruta storefront with WooCommerce managing catalogue, customers, stock, shipping and orders.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React, TypeScript and Vite frontend in this repository; static hosting and PHP integration on the existing Hostinger plan. Preserve live WooCommerce data and original category navigation order. English, INR, India-only addresses, guest checkout, accounts, wishlist and verified order tracking. Production payment submission stays disabled until gateway testing. Shipping fee, free-delivery threshold, return policy and verified WhatsApp number remain launch inputs.

## Brand Commitments

Original Saalankruta logo and recognisable pink and gold. User explicitly requests royal luxury, liquid glass effects and navigation informed by navbar.gallery. All other interface design may change.

## Evidence on Hand

143 published product records and 24 category records in public catalogue snapshots. Original product images and logo. No verified testimonials, material certifications or popularity claims.

## Product Principles

Keep product appearance accurate. Make purchase decisions understandable. Keep customer and order access private. Preserve the catalogue and business records through migration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard controls, readable contrast, reduced motion, touch targets and no horizontal overflow from 320 to 1440 pixels. Mobile Safari and Chrome checks required before launch.
